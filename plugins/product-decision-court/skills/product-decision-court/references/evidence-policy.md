# Evidence policy

## Tiers

### AUDITED_GOLD

Use the principle, causal mechanism, landmine, tradeoff, and source list. Attribute only the audited claim. Paraphrase. Do not reproduce transcript passages.

### PROVISIONAL_CLUSTER

Use only to discover a possible lens, disagreement, or diagnostic question. Do not claim consensus. Do not state that a named guest endorsed synthesized wording. Confirm against audited evidence before strong attribution.

## Rules

- Diagnose before retrieving precedents.
- Prefer a relevant minority view over generic high-frequency advice.
- Treat frequency as recurrence, not truth.
- Separate a guest's observation from the court's inference.
- Preserve counterpoints and stage-dependent disagreements.
- Use at most five precedents in a medium answer.
- Omit attribution when it does not sharpen the decision.
- Never expose or reproduce the transcript archive.
